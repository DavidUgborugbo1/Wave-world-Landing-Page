The Rides

Pick your pace.

From gut-drop speed slides to a river you can float for hours — every ride is sorted by how hard your heart will work.

Kraken's Plunge

A near-vertical 60ft drop into total darkness before the tube spits you into the splash basin. Not for the faint of heart.

High Intensity

Riptide Racers

Eight side-by-side mat lanes. Race your whole crew head-to-head down a 400ft straightaway — bragging rights included.

Group Favorite

Tidal Bowl

A four-person raft swirls the rim of a 50ft funnel before dropping into the pool. Loud, wet, and better with friends.

Family Ride

Lazy Lagoon

Half a mile of slow-moving river through palm cover and mist fans. Grab a tube and do absolutely nothing for as long as you like.

All Ages
