Frequently Asked Questions

Got ticket questions?

🎟 How do I receive my wristband?

Upon completing checkout, an electronic barcode ticket is immediately sent to your email. At the park gate, scan it at any turnstile or kiosk to receive your waterproof wristband.

🌧 What is the Rain Guarantee policy?

If extreme weather closes the rides for more than 90 consecutive minutes during your visit, we will issue a complimentary return day pass valid for the rest of the season.

👶 Do young children need admission?

Children aged 2 and under enter completely free! Life jackets and toddler splash lagoon zones are provided at no charge.

🔁 Can I transfer or reschedule my tickets?

Yes! Tickets can be rescheduled for any available date in the current season up to 24 hours before your selected visit date.
